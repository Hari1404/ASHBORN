## GOAL
Until now the tags in the Edit tags box are plain rows that you cannot press: a tag you added stayed forever. This packet adds the two missing things. (1) Every tag in the box shows its name and two small buttons, `Rename` and `Remove`. `Rename` turns that row into a field with `Save` and `Cancel`. `Remove` turns that row into a question with two buttons, `Remove` and `Keep`, because a wrong tap on a phone is easy and a removed tag comes back only by typing its name again. (2) Errors (a name that is empty, too long, or already taken) show in one line inside the row you were working in, and after an error the focus goes back to where you work. Removing a tag does NOT delete your old sessions: they keep the tag. Renaming changes the name on your old sessions too (they point to the tag, not to its name). Nothing in the database changes, and no other file than the 13 of the table.

## WHAT THE NEW CODE DOES (for the owner and the later packets)
- **`useTimer.ts` (the hook).** Two new functions, `renameTag(id, name)` and `archiveTag(id)`. Both answer `{ ok: true }` or `{ ok: false, message }` with the plain words of `problemText`, both never throw, both read the tag list again after the call (if the list cannot be read, the screen puts it right by itself: the renamed tag in place, the removed tag out), and neither ever chooses a tag. If the removed tag was the one picked before Start, the pick goes back to `No tag`.
- **`TagManage.tsx` (the Edit tags box).** The rows described in the GOAL, one open row at a time (opening another row, `Back`, closing the box or the dark layer drops an open row and sends nothing), while a call runs every button of the box is off and the fields are read-only, errors in one line inside the row (with `role="alert"`), and the focus rules: into the field after a refused rename (your text stays), onto `Keep` after a refused removal, into the Add field after a refused Add or when the row is gone, and back on the `Rename` button of the same row after Save or Cancel. If a row's tag has left the list (for example it was removed on another device), the row disappears by itself and its words show under the Add field. Escape inside a row cancels that row only.

## WHAT THE PLANNER HAD TO DECIDE (read them and say which are wrong; all are DEFAULT)
1. `Rename` and `Remove` are two small outlined buttons at the end of each row; the name is plain text and cannot be pressed (a name must never look like a button).

5. An error shows inside the row you were working in (not under the Add field, which can be far away on a phone) and stays until you type, or press Cancel or Keep.
6. Removing means archiving (rule 5 of `DECISIONS_TAGS.md`): old sessions keep the tag, and adding the same name again brings the SAME tag back. Renaming changes the name on old sessions too (rule 6). A tag may be renamed or removed while a session with it runs on another device; the line in the dial follows the name at the next reading.
7. The words (all DEFAULT): `Rename`, `Remove`, `Save`, `Cancel`, `Keep`, `Remove "NAME"?`, `Old sessions keep it. Adding the same name again brings it back.` The error words are the ones that already exist (`problemText`).
8.
